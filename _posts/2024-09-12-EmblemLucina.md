# **Introduction**
Lucina represents the 1st of the 3DS Fire Emblem Games: **Awakening**. She is a Support-focused Emblem and shows why Support Abilities can be just as powerful as combat ones, just like Micaiah. However, Lucina carves out a niche for herself by focusing on Chain Attacks and Chain Guard, two mechanics that, when used correctly, can have devastating results.

## Quick Refresher: Chain Attacks and Chain Guard

<details>
<summary> Chain Attack </summary>

Chain Attacks are exclusive to <b>Backup Units</b> such as Fighters, Warriors, Heroes, etc. When a Unit initiates combat against an enemy, all allied Backup Units adjacent to the target (or 2 spaces away if equipped with a Ranged Weapon like a Hand Axe or Javelin) will join in and attack the same enemy before the main Unit attacks. Chain Attacks deal fixed damage equal to 10% of the target's maximum HP, ignoring Defense and Resistance, at a fixed 80% accuracy.

</details>


<details>
<summary> Chain Guard </summary>

Chain Guard is exclusive to <b>Qi Adept</b> Classes such as Martial Monks/Masters, Dancers, and Enchanters. This ability requires the user to be at full HP. When activated, if an ally adjacent to a Chain Guarding Unit is attacked, the first attack from the enemy will be blocked by the Qi Adept Unit at the cost of 20% of their maximum HP. Once a Qi Adept has blocked an attack, they cannot do it again for the rest of the Enemy Phase. In other words, Chain Guard is "Once per Enemy Phase."

</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Dual Strike</b> is Lucina's signature Sync Skill and one of the more powerful ones in the game. It lets any Unit, regardless of Class Type, perform Chain Attacks as if they were a Backup Unit. This essentially turns Lucina's bearer into a Pseudo-Backup Unit. In my personal opinion, you can never have too many Backup Units in your army. Since Backup Units can Chain Attack naturally, Dual Strike is completely useless on them. It's a good idea to avoid putting Lucina on them if possible. <br><br>

<b>Dual Assist</b> patches up the biggest weakness of Backup Units and Chain Attacks. Normally, Chain Attacks can only be performed on enemies either adjacent or 2 spaces away from the enemy. Dual Assist greatly expands the Chain Attack range of Backup Units. In simple terms, if a Backup Unit has Dual Assist, ANY enemy they can reach normally by moving, is susceptible to being hit by a Chain Attack. That 35% activation rate might seem low in theory, but in practice, you will be pleasantly surprised at how often Dual Assist triggers during a Chapter. <br><br>

Chain Attacks are incredibly useful as they allow you to squeeze in just a little more damage to one-round an enemy. 


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

I make no exaggerations when I say <b>Bonded Shield</b> is one of the best Engage Skills in the base game. This skill was heavily slept on and underrated during Engage's initial release, but nowadays its power has been more widely recognized. <br><br>

On paper, Bonded Shield just looks like a watered-down version of standard Chain Guard. Chain Guard has a 100% activation rate compared to Bonded Shield's 80%. However, that is until you realize that Bonded Shield lacks the 2 biggest weaknesses of Chain Guard. <br>

<ul>

<li><b>Bonded Shield doesn't require Lucina's bearer to be at full HP.</b></li><br>

<li><b>It isn't limited to once per Enemy Phase. The moment you use it, it will stay active for the entire Enemy Phase.</b></li><br>

</ul>

Bonded Shield has two general use cases. <br>

<ul>
<li><b>Rushing a squad of up to 4 Units into the middle of enemy lines, completely immune to damage for the entire Enemy Phase.</b></li><br>

<li><b>Baiting out dangerous foes by putting someone in their attack range and having their attack blocked by Bonded Shield. </b></li><br>
</ul>

Bonded Shield's biggest weakness is its base 80% Activation Rate. This means that there is a 1 in 5 chance of it failing. However, that's where the Class Bonuses come into the equation to rig the odds in your favor. <br><br>

For Cavalry, Armored, and Flying Units, their 100% Activation Rate only works for allies of the same Unit Type. Say, for example, Lucina's bearer is a Cavalry Unit and uses Bonded Shield next to two allies. Ally A is a Cavalry Unit while Ally B is a Backup Unit. Only Ally A will have the 100% Activation Rate while Ally B has the standard 80%. If your army is heavy on Cavalry, or Flying Units, it may be worth leveraging these bonuses, especially Fliers as Bonded Shield can bait out dangerous Bow enemies. <br><br>

But if you don't feel like dealing with all that RNG, just put Lucina on a Qi Adept Unit. They have a <b>100%</b> Activation Rate for Bonded Shield for ALL Unit Types, no questions asked. Qi Adepts have been getting the short end of the stick with Engage Skill bonuses, but Bonded Shield more than makes up for that as they get the best deal bar none. <br><br>

Lastly, please remember that Bonded Shield, though powerful, has its limitations. <br>

<ul>

<li><b>Bonded Shield only blocks the FIRST attack of an enemy. If the enemy is doubling, you're on your own for the second attack.</b></li><br>

<li><b>Bonded Shield does not work on Lucina's bearers themselves. This means that they are vulnerable to enemies with ranged weapons who might target them, instead of their allies.</b></li> 
</ul>

</details>

## Engage Attack

| ![Lodestar Rush Pic]({{ site.baseurl }}/img/lucina/FE17_All_for_One_Icon.webp) <br> **All for One** | Use to make a Sword attack against an adjacent foe. All allies within 2 spaces Chain Attack.  |

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Unfortunately for Lucina, the cost of having one of the best Engage Skills in the game is having the 2nd worst Engage Attack, <b>All For One</b>. I say 2nd worst because nothing can be worse than Leif's Quadruple Hit and all of its baggage. All For One has several things holding it back. <br>

<ul>

<li>You only make a regular attack with a sword and Lucina's kit has no way of increasing your offensive power.</li><br> 

<li>The main power of this attack relies on having multiple allies within 2 spaces of Lucina's bearer. So unless you have a lot of your army bunched up together, don't expect more than 2, maybe 3 allies to contribute Chain Attacks. </li><br> 

<li>The Chain Attacks triggered by One For All still have the fixed 80% Hit Rate of regular Chain Attacks. This means that if any of them miss, you will do less damage than the Combat Forecast predicted. </li>
</ul>

However, All For One is not useless. Because it's an Engage Attack, the enemy cannot counterattack. Use this to soften up bosses so the rest of your army will have an easier time finishing them off. <br><br>

Another thing to remember is that All For One interacts with Dual Assist. If you have any Backup Units equipped with Dual Assist, there is a chance they will also contribute Chain Attacks to Lucina's One For All, even if they are not in range of the effect but are in range to strike the enemy if Dual Assist would activate normally. <br><br>

The Class Bonuses are rather meh. Dragons getting guaranteed Chain Attack accuracy is the only one worth considering as I do not recommend putting Lucina on Backup Units as that's a waste of Dual Strike. <br><br>

All For One is a mixed bag across the board but this isn't necessarily a bad thing. As stated previously, Lucina is a Support Emblem, not a Combat Emblem. But, if you want to use Lucina more offensively, you'll get more mileage from her Engage Weapons.


</details>

<b>Parthia</b> is definitely Lucina's best Engage Weapon as its Double EXP effect makes it Marth's Mercurius in Bow form. Furthermore, that 17 Might makes Parthia excellent for shooting down Flying Enemies as it becomes 51 Might with Weapon Effectiveness. The +5 Resistance is the cherry on top as it can make Lucina's bearer a decent Mage Killer. Lastly, remember that Parthia is a Bond 10 Weapon, meaning you don't even have to complete Lucina's Paralogue to have it. However, you still should do her Paralogue for the rewards it gives.

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Dual Support</b> is Lucina's final Sync Skill and it's a pretty interesting one. Its main purpose is patching up one of the Bonded Shield's weaknesses by making Lucina's bearer a better dodge tank. This skill is especially fantastic if Alear is Lucina's main user as not only do they support EVERY playable character in the game, but they are also the only character capable of having an S Support with someone. <br><br>

How Dual Support calculates the Avoid Bonus is much simpler than you'd think. It takes the Support Level Lucina's bearer has with an adjacent unit and assigns a value to that Level. It then multiplies the Level by 5. For example, say that Alear is Synced with Lucina. They are surrounded by 4 Units. Alear has an S, A, B, and C Rank Support with these 4 Units. Doing the math, that would be <b>(6*5) +  (4*5) + (3*5) + (2*5)</b> for a total of <b>+75 Avoid</b>. <br><br>

Dual Support might be good, but the real prize for doing Lucina's Paralogue is <b>Dual Assist+</b> which doubles the Activation Rate from 35% to 70%. This means that Lucina's bearer (and anyone who inherits this Skill) will Chain Attack almost constantly. This essentially means that enemies within Dual Assist's range effectively have at least 10% less HP. This damage really stacks over the course of a Chapter. 

</details>

# **Character Builds**

When running Lucina, you can focus on maximizing either her offensive capabilities via Dual Strike or her defensive abilities via Bonded Shield.

<details>
<summary> Lucina, Princess of Poison</summary>

One of the best ways of leveraging Dual Strike is on a <b>Knife User</b>. For a refresher, Knives in Engage are capable of inflicting Poison on the target if the attack deals damage. Poison is a static Debuff that adds "True Damage" to your attacks on the afflicted enemy. Each stack of Poison adds +1 Damage to your attacks, up to a maximum of 3 stacks for +3 Damage. This applies to Chain Attacks inflicted by a Knife. This build exploits this mechanic to its full potential. <br><br>

By the time you obtain Lucina, you are entering a phase of the game where Enemy Bosses will have multiple health bars. Knife Chain Attacks not only pile on the damage, but also stacks Poison to increase the damage as the battle rages on. Do this and you will be amazed at how fast enemy HP bars disintegrate as you keep fighting. The Boss will constantly take an extra +2 to +3 damage from every hit. <br><br> 

This build can work on Thieves, but from my experience, you'll get even better results with a <b>Wolf Knight</b>. Not only is Wolf Knight a fantastic combat class with their speed and decent bulk. Furthermore, they are a Cavalry Class meaning they have increased movement for extra range with Dual Assist and their Class Bonus with Bonded Shield. Lastly, Wolf Knight is not a difficult class to obtain as it only requires Knife Proficiency (Obtainable from Micaiah or Leif) and either Sword, Lance, or Axe Proficiency. Sword Proficiency is the most common Proficiency in the game as from the 12 Base Game Emblems alone, 11 of them grant Sword Proficiency. 

</details>

<details>
<summary> Kung-Fu Bodyguard</summary>

If you want to maximize the defensive utility of Bonded Shield, your best option is with a <b>Qi Adept as they have a 100% Bonded Shield Activation Rate</b>. When you first obtain Lucina, your only options for this build are <b>Framme, Jean, and Alear</b>. The latter two are Qi Adepts (Martial Monks) by default while Alear starts with Arts Proficiency and only needs Staff Proficiency from Micaiah or Leif to reclass into a Qi Adept. <br><br>

Alear is a very popular candidate for this build as not only do they support everyone in the game to maximize the effects of Dual Support, but their Personal Skill Divinely Inspiring (Adjacent Allies deal +3 Damage and take 1 less Damage) synergizes incredibly well with Bonded Shield. <br><br>

However, once you gain access to Arts Proficiency through the Ring of the Instructor, you can run this build on anyone reclassed into a Qi Adept Class as Alear performs well on other Emblems besides Lucina. 

</details>


# **Final Thoughts** <br>
Lucina is an Emblem that quickly became one of my favorites because she is so versatile. You can focus on Bonded Shield to protect your army during the Enemy Phase or focus on her Chain Attack utility to shred through enemies during the Player Phase. As someone who usually runs at least 3-4 Back Units in my army, her Dual Assist skills are a godsend for dramatically increasing their utility. Lucina definitely carves out a niche for herself without stepping on the toes of other Support-focused Emblems. You should not sleep on her as her abilities are very much worth using.
