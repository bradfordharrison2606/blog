# **Introduction**
Camilla stands out among Emblems in both the Base Game and the DLC. She is the only truly Flier-focused Emblem the player has access to. Unlike her sister Corrin, Camilla is an Emblem that focuses more on offense both physically and magically, allowing her bearer to function as a decent mixed attacker. She also comes with terrain manipulation abilities like Corrin.

Wearing the Bracelet of the Doting Sister provided boosts to HP, Speed, and Resistance. The most notable boost is Speed as Camilla is tied with Lyn for the highest passive Speed boost provided by an Emblem. Having more Speed is never a bad thing as it lets you hit doubling thresholds for more damage and increases your evasive capabilities. Since this Bracelet doesn't directly boost Strength or Magic, it's a good idea to put it on someone who already has good offensive abilities.

Like Corrin, I'm giving <b>Dragon Vein</b> its own section. If you're familiar with Corrin's Dragon Vein, then Camilla's version functions nearly identically. Where Camiila differs is that she offers some unique terrain effects compared to Corrin. Also, Camilla tends to swap the "utility" of her Veins compared to Corrin. What I mean is that if Corrin used a Dragon Vein effect Offensively, then Camilla would use that same effect Defensively, and vice versa. This means that just like with Corrin, you'll need to familiarize yourself with how Camilla's terrain effects work and how they are placed. Basically where Corrin Zigs, Camilla Zags.

Furthermore, please remember that (just like Corrin) Camilla's terrain created through Dragon Vein DOES NOT DISCRIMINATE. This means whatever terrain you make will affect both sides equally. The positive effects can help the enemy and the negative effects can harm you, the Player. Like Corrin, the terrain effect Dragon Vein grants is determined by the Unit Type of whoever is wearing her bracelet. Below is a table detailing the different Dragon Vein effects.

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Most of the effects Camilla gives out are similar to Corrin's except for two key differences. The <b>Smoke</b> created by Covert Units is useful both offensively and defensively. If you have some bulky enemies coming at you in a group, you can throw down some Smoke to severely weaken them. This is especially useful on Maddening compared to Corrin's Fog as enemies either have such ludicrously high hit rates that dodge-tanking isn't a viable strategy so it would be better to kill them on the Player Phase. As a bonus, the Smoke slows down enemy movement a little bit, though Flames are usually better for that. <br><br>

The <b>Ice Tiles</b> created by Qi Adepts are a marked improvement over the Ice Pillars Corrin gave to Qi Adepts. You can place these under allies who haven't moved yet to give them that extra bit of mobility. Sigurd always benefits from more movement due to Momentum. Lastly, while it might seem strange that whenever Camilla creates Flame Tiles, it surrounds her user, including the space they are occupying. However, Camilla has a Sync Skill that turns this into a benefit. 

</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Decisive Strike</b> is a bit of a niche skill. The extra damage inflicted after combat CAN kill targets who survive a Critical Hit so this skill is one of the few ways to bypass skills like Hold Out. Despite this, Decisive Strike is a bit meh as unless you're fighting something bulky like a General, a Critical Hit will usually be enough to finish the job. <br><br>

<b>Detoxify</b> is Simple but Practical. The ability to heal Poison is great to have because Engage loves to throw enemy Knife Users (Thieves, Wolf Knights) at you en masse, especially in the later chapters and those stacks of Poison can be a death sentence if you don't cleanse them. I find this skill to be similar to Tiki's Lifesphere as while you don't have to select Wait to trigger it, you don't heal HP and Detoxify only works on Poison. <br><br>

<b>Groundswell</b> is a skill I both love and hate. The ability to cleanse harmful terrain and recover a bit of HP is incredibly useful, especially when you're dealing with the Flames generated by Camilla's Dragon Vein or more importantly, Miasma of Chapters 15 and 19. For a refresher, Miasma is the single most dangerous Terrain effect in Engage because, unlike other effects, Miasma does discriminate. If an ally Unit is standing in Miasma, they lose 20 DEF and RES, but if an Enemy stands in Miasma, they GAIN 20 DEF and RES making them nigh unkillable. Groundswell is a good counter to this but it sucks that this skill is locked behind a DLC Paywall. 


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

I find Camilla's <b>Soar</b> to be a nice counterpart to Sigurd's Gallop. Both Engage Skills increase movement but Camilla spices things up by granting Flying Movement to her bearer. What this means is that while you're Engaged with Camilla, you have the same freedom of movement as regular Flying Units. This allows non-Flying Units the ability to traverse terrain such as Water, Mountains, and other Flier-only terrain. The cherry on top is that even though Soar makes you a Pseudo-Flier Unit, you do not gain any of the usual weaknesses of Flier Units (Bows and Wind Magic). Soar is also a bit of a double-edged sword when it comes to terrain effects. Being a Flying Unit means you aren't harmed by terrain like Flames or Smoke but it also means that while Soar is active, you don't benefit from positive terrain effects, like those created by Corrin or Camilla herself, so it's a trade-off. <br><br>

For the Class Bonuses, Dragons deal damage splash damage to all foes within a certain radius of themself, a reference to the Savage Blow skill from Camilla's Malig Knight Class back in Fates. On Alear this is useful for AoE Chip Damage which can be enough to put an enemy into the kill range of another ally. Cavalry and Flying Units get additional movement on top of what Soar already gives. Personally, I find Camilla wasted on Flying Units because the main appeal of Soar is the unrestricted movement that Flying Units have by default. So I often find myself putting Camilla on Cavalry Units for the MOV+3 and Flying Mobility. 

</details>

Mystical Units dealing extra damage can be nice, especially when combined with Camilla's 1st Engage weapon while Qi Adepts adding Healing Glow Tiles adjacent to them is alright, nothing special. <details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>
Camilla's Engage Weapons are pretty good across the board and have something for both Physical and Magical Units. <br><br>

The <b>Bolt Axe</b> is great for a Bond 1 Engage Weapon as Magical Weapons such as the Levin Sword and Radiant Bow are great in Engage. The Bolt Axe in particular is especially nice as the only magical axe in Engage outside DLC is the Hurricane Axe which has better Might and Hit compared to the Bolt Axe, but it is also much heavier and cannot double attack. Thus the Bolt Axe greatly outclasses the Hurricane Axe. That -20 to Avoid penalty is a pain but I find it to be a reasonable tradeoff. <br><br>

<b>Lightning</b> at Bond 10 might look weak but it makes up for it by being a Brave Weapon, meaning that you strike twice in a row when initiating combat. The only other Brave Tome in Engage is the S-Rank Tome Nova which you don't get until the endgame. <br><br>

<b>Camilla's Axe</b> at Bond 15 is another fantastic Engage Weapon as it is statistically identical to the S-Rank Axe, except it is much lighter. That effect of dealing damage equal to the difference between the foe's RES and DEF might seem worthless until you remember that Mages in Engage have sky-high Resistance Stats but also low Defence Stats. For reference, Sages in Engage have a maximum Resistance and Defence of 44 and 20 respectively. This means Camilla's Axe will deal an extra 24 Damage to them, 48 if you're doubling. Combine this with the +10 RES this weapon grants and you have another Mage-Killer weapon like Sigurd's Tyrfing.


</details>

There is a degree of risk with this Engrave since that -20 to Avoid and Dodge makes you more vulnerable to Enemy Criticals. However, if you're using defensive abilities like Chain Guard, Bonded Shield, etc, you won't have to worry too much about this. <br><br>

Corrin's Engrave is the only one that gives +30 Critical but it is also much safer since it boosts your Avoid by 10 and your Dodge by 30 in exchange for -2 Might. When making a Crit Build, it's a matter of personal preference between the two.

Despite Dragon not being inheritable, like Corrin, Camilla has much more to offer your army compared to her sister. <br><br>

The main prizes here are <b>Groundswell and Detoxify</b>. The ability to cleanse dangerous terrain and recover HP is quite useful, especially in certain chapters. Detoxify is very useful one as it effectively renders you immune to Poison as it will always be cleansed by the end of your turn. Both of these skills are rather cheap so feel free to spread them (especially Groundswell) around if you like. <br><br>

<b>Decisive Strike</b> could see some use on Crit Builds if you want an insurance policy in case something survives a Critical Hit. Camilla's Engrave even greatly boosts Critical Rate.  <br><br>

Camilla's Stat Boost is <b>Speed/Resistance+</b>. These skills are nice and decently priced for the Speed boost they offer. I don't find people use these skills much. If you want a Speed boost, you could go to Lyn for that. The Resistance boost seems a bit tacked on all things considered. <br><br>

Camilla's Guard Skill is <b>Lance Guard</b> which can be useful as not only is it cheap, but Engage has a lot of Lance-using Enemies who hit like trucks, especially with Greatlances. It's never a bad idea to have a buffer against that kind of damage. <br><br>

Lastly, Camilla grants <b>Axe and Tome Proficiency</b>. Axe Proficiency is always great as that leads to some of the best classes in Engage while Tome Proficiency is something you'll have to work for, just like with Soren. If you want Tome Proficiency, it's best to get this from either Celica or Micaiah. 

</details>



# **Character Builds**

Like other DLC Emblems, I don't have much experience with Camilla. However, I can say that she works well with either Physical or Magical Units. I lean toward pairing Camilla with Mixed Attackers.

<details>
<summary> Mage Knight Build </summary>

Mage Knight is one of the better clashes in Engage due to its versatility with both physical and magical attacks. Since Mage Knights are Cavalry, they receive the MOV+2 bonus from Camilla's Soar. <br><br>

Depending on the character in question, Camilla can make them into a powerhouse Mixed Attacker who can switch between physical and magical damage depending on the target. Mage Knights have good Resistance Stats which when paired with Camilla's Axe, makes them excellent Mage Killers as they will barely take damage while completely annihilating their target in response. <br><br>

I recommend inheriting a Speed+ Skill from Lyn to combine with Camilla's passive boost to Speed. All of this Speed will not only make it easier to meet doubling thresholds but also make it easier to Quadruple Attack with Lightning. Even with its low Might, attacking 4 Times will rack up the damage. 


</details>


# **Final Thoughts** <br>
As an Emblem Camilla's biggest asset (pun fully intended) is her ability to make anyone into a Flying Unit with added movement. That isn't her only trick as she provided great offences with her Engage Weapons and Engage Attack. Considering that Camilla's Divine Paralogue is considered by many players to be the most difficult to complete, especially if you wait until the endgame, this level of power and utility feels like a fitting reward. Many players consider Camilla a side-grade to Sigurd (like Hector to Ike) and I find this to be a compliment as Sigurd is one of the better Emblems in the game.
