# **Introduction**
Chrom & Robin are similar to the Emblems of The Sacred and Rivals. They are two Emblems for the price of one. However, unlike the Emblems of the Sacred and Rivals, Chrom is the star here while Robin is sort of tagging along for the ride. Chrom & Robin are Emblem I would describe as an "All Rounder". They have a varied and diverse skillset with something both Physical and Magical Units will find use for. However, because they spread themselves so thin, very few Units will benefit from their entire kit.

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Surprise Attack</b> is made for Covert Units as the game heavily encourages you to put in Terrain with Avoid bonuses. Covert Units have this advantage negated by Mystical Units who ignore Terrain Bonuses when using Magic, so completely canceling the opponent's counterattack patches up this weakness. On Thieves, this is especially useful as it will allow them to attack and poison enemies without fear of retaliation. <br><br>

<b>Rally Spectrum</b> and its upgraded version are great as it's basically the same as Byleth's Instruct when used by a Dragon Unit. However, unlike Byleth where you have to be Engaged with him, you can use Rally Spectrum anytime you want provided you are either synced with Chrom & Robin or have inherited this skill. I cannot emphasize how much +3 to all of your basic stats is as that could let you one-round enemies with the added power or meet doubling thresholds. <br><br>

<b>Brute Force</b> is another good Sync Skill. The game says "critical hits deal increased damage.". What this means is that instead of dealing 3x Damage, you instead deal 4x Damage. I find this skill to be overkill because usually, normal Critical Hits are enough to kill a foe. However, on higher difficulties, enemies can be bulky enough that the 4x Damage is enough to kill them. <br><br>

Finally, <b>Charm</b> is a Sync Skill that is simple but incredibly effective. As a refresher, Chain Attacks have a fixed accuracy of 80%, meaning that most of the time they will land but not always. Increasing their accuracy to 90% makes Chain Attacks even more reliable for tacking just a little more damage onto enemies. Please remember that Charm only affects Chain Attacks from other Units and not the Unit themself.  <br><br>


</details>

<summary> Explanation </summary>

When you Engage with Chrom & Robin, you activate <b>Other Half</b> where Robin himself finally gets to strut his stuff. While Engaged, Robin will act as a Backup Unit always Chain Attack in your combats. However, just like Lyn's Doubles, Robin will only Chain Attack for his bearer, no one else. Keep in mind that Charm at Bond 13 affects Robin's Chain Attack Accuracy as well. <br><br>

Like Sigurd's Gallop and Roy's Rise Above, this skill's second effect is simple but incredibly practical as that +10 Magic goes a long way. Now, in theory, this Skill is designed to make Physical Units capable of dealing decent Damage with Robin's Engage Weapons, both of which are Magic-based. However, in practice, there is nothing stopping you from putting their Bracelet on a Magical Unit and boosting their already high Magic Stat through the roof when Engaged. This is how most players choose to run Chrom & Robin as making an already powerful mage even stronger will get you much more value compared to making a physical unit mediocre with magic attacks. <br><br>

For the Class Bonuses, Backup Units getting 100% Chain Attack means every enemy they fight while Engaged will have 10% less HP while Covert Units gain additional movement while Engaged. Dragons, however, get the best deal and no, it's not Robin Chain Attacking twice. I'll go over this in the Character Build Section but putting Chron & Robin on a Dragon allows some hilarious stuff to happen. For now, let's move on to their Engage Attack and ho boy, it is quite a lot to unpack.

# Engage Attack

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Giga Levin Sword is an oddball compared to other Engage Attacks as it is a bit more complicated under the hood. This Engage Attack can be either extremely powerful or extremely weak and it depends on the Wielder and what the target is. The description of this skill is a trick to understand and requires some explanation. <br><br>

How GLS works is it uses the character's Physical Attack Power (their Strength Stat + their Weapon's Might) and uses the enemy's Resistance Stat for damage calculations. The following is an example of how this works.  <br><br>

You have a unit, Amber Engaged with Chrom & Robin at Bond 1 and equipped with a normal, unforged Silver Sword with the following parameters. <br>

<table>
  <tr>
    <td></td>
    <td>Base STR</td>
    <td>Weapon Might</td>
    <td>Physical Attack Power</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <th>Amber  </th>
    <td>41 </td>
    <td>12 </td>
    <td><b>53</b> </td>
  </tr>
</table>

With these stats, Amber's Giga Levin Sword will have a raw attack power of 53 Damage. <br><br>

There are two enemies within attack range. A General with 14 Resistance and a Sage with 36 Resistance. Amber uses GLS on both of them and the Combat Forecast predicts he will deal the following damage to each enemy.  <br><br>

<table>
  <tr>
    <td></td>
    <th>Resistance (RES)</th>
    <th>Damage Taken (53 - RES) </th>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <th>General  </th>
    <td> 14 </td>
    <td> <b>39</b> </td>
  </tr>
    <tr>
    <th>Sage  </th>
    <td> 36 </td>
    <td> <b>17</b> </td>
  </tr>
</table>


As you can see, the General is the one taking significantly more damage due to his much lower Resistance Stat. Thus to get the most out of GLS, it is best to use it with a High STR character on enemies with Low RES such as Armored Units. As always, if you want to pump up that damage, consider using GLS with Steel or Silver Blades.  <br><br>

Lastly, it doesn't matter if you are using a Physical Sword or a Magical Sword like Robin's Levin Sword. The damage will always be calculated using the wielder's Strength Stat.  <br><br>

The Class Bonuses are pretty nice as they are all about dealing more damage with this attack. Mystical Units especially benefit as adding half of their MAG stat to the damage can be a way to pump out good damage numbers with GLS. Alternatively, you could consider GLS as another way of exterminating Armored Foes, especially if you use an Armorslayer with it.  <br><br>

As always with DLC Emblems, GLS has an upgraded + version. GLS+ requires an ally wearing Lucina's Ring to be adjacent to Chrom & Robin's bearer. This will allow all adjacent allies to Chain Attack before striking with GLS. Given Lucina must be adjacent for this to happen, this means that at minimum, you will receive one (1) Chain Attack. Lastly, like Lucina's One For All, there is the possibility for Backup Units with Dual Assist(+) to also participate in the Chain Attacks, even if they are not adjacent to Chrom & Robin's Bearer. 


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

When it comes to the duo's Engage Weapons, the first two come from Robin while the last one comes from Chrom. <br><br>

Robin's <b>Levin Sword</b> is another oddball. As we've seen with Sigurd's Ridersbane, Lyn's Killer Bow, Ike's Hammer, and Roy's Wyrmslayer, whenever an Emblem gives you an Engage Weapon versopm of a standard weapon, the Engage Weapon is usually stronger/better in some aspect. This is the exception as Robin's Levin Sword is inferior to a regular Levin Sword in everything but Weight. If you already have a Levin Sword in your inventory during battle, there is no reason to use Robin's version unless it allows you to double due to being lighter. <br><br>


Robin's <b>Thoron</b> isn't worse than a regular Thoron, just different. Robin's Thoron is treated as a regular Tome, meaning it can only attack from 1-2 Range but it can double. While less powerful and accurate than vanilla Thoron, Robin's Thoron is significantly lighter, decreasing the chances of getting doubled by enemies on the Enemy Phase. <br><br>

Finally, the only Engage Weapon we get from Chrom is his version of the iconic <b>Falchion</b>. Compared to Marth's version (Falchion) and Lucina's version (Parallel Falchion), Chrom's Falchion has the highest base Might at 13 but is also the heaviest with a Weight of 12. Again, Dragon Effectiveness (without help from the 13th Emblem) isn't anything to write home about but it is useful against all the Phantom Wyverns you encounter in the Divine Paralogues. <br><br>


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Chrom & Robin's Skill Inheritance is quite diverse like their skill set and while a tad expensive, each of them provides great value. <br><br>

<b>Rally Spectrum</b> is the most universally useful Inheritable Skill these two have to offer. Rally Spectrum+ at 2000 SP isn't that outrageously expensive as you can easily get that with some Skill Books from the well. The problem is grinding someone all the way to Bond 18. Normally, I find Rally Spectrum on Chrom & Robin's main bearer to be enough. <br><br>

<b>Brute Force</b> is fantastic if you're running Critical Hit Builds on Physical Units, especially those with subpar STR Stats as that 4x multiplier to Critical Hits will more than make up for it. Bond 8 and 1500 SP is a fair price to pay to make your Crits hit even harder than normal. <br><br>

<b>Charm</b> is a skill I don't highly recommend. If you're using a lot of Backup Units (and Lucina) with Dual Assist, then it may be worth it. 800 SP while decently priced, isn't enough to outweigh that Bond 13 requirement. If you're using the character Boucheron, consider putting this skill on him as his Personal Skill gives him bonuses whenever someone Chain Attacks in his combat. <br><br>

Last of the Inheritable Sync Skills, <b>Surprise Attack</b> is the most niche. If you're running a lot of Covert Units (Thieves, Snipers, Tireur d'élite) then spreading this around is nice. If you like to use the Fog from Corrin's Dragon Veins, I guess spreading this around could also be useful if you can stomach that 3000 SP price tag. <br><br>

Moving on, Chrom & Robin's Stat Skill is <b>Speed/Dexterity+</b> which is pretty nice. However, if you're looking for Speed or Dexterity boosts, it's cheaper to get them from Lyn and Lucina respectively.<br><br>

Their Weapon Skill is <b>Sword Guard</b> which is rather meh. Swords aren't that deadly in the hands of the enemy so the only way I see this being used is against Swordmasters who could potentially double you. <br><br>

Lastly, Chrom & Robin grant <b>Sword and Tome Proficiency</b>. As you'd expect from DLC Emblems, Tome Proficiency is locked off until Bond 16. If you want Tome Proficiency for cheap, Cecilia and Micaiah are your best options.  

</details>



# **Character Builds**



<details>
<summary> My Better Half </summary>
This is the most standard way of running Chrom & Robin. You ignore the Physical aspects of their kit and focus on the Magical aspects, mainly the +10 MAG from Other Half and Robin's Engage Weapons. This build works best on Mystical Units as they all have great Magic stats and benefit from getting increased damage from Giga Levin Sword.

</details>

<details>
<summary> Mage Knights and Griffin Knights stay Winning </summary>

The problem with Chrom & Robin's kit is that very few Units and Classes can take advantage of their entire skill set. Mage Knights and Griffin Knights are the exception as they are classes who function decently well as Mixed-Attackers. They benefit from Other Half boosting their raw Magic damage output while also having the physical capabilities to make good use of Brute Force through Killer Weapons and Giga Levin Sword. Griffin Knights especially as they get a Class Bonus from GLS by being Flying Units. If you are going this route, it's best to use it on characters with decent STR stats as Other Half can mix mediocre MAG stats. 

</details>


<details>
<summary> Brute Force + Vantage + Wrath = DEAD ENEMY </summary>
If you want to use Chrom & Robin on Physical Units, it's best to focus on maximizing the power from Brute Force. I suggest putting a Critical Boosting Engrave  (Lyn, Eirika, Corrin, Camilla, The 13th Emblem) on a Killer Weapon to pump up your Critical Rate as much as possible. If you're using this build on Chrom & Robin's main bearer, it can be a good idea to try the Vantage+Wrath combo on them in their 2 Skill Slots. Wrath will boost your Critical Rate even higher when injured and Vantage will ensure that you strike first on the Enemy Phase, provided your HP is at a certain value.

</details>


<details>
<summary> Robin == Alfred?  </summary>
As you know, the primary effect of Other Half is that Robin will Chain Attack in your combat while you are Engaged, and if you're a Dragon Unit, Robin Chain Attacks twice. However, did you know that this effect also extends to the character <b>Alfred</b>, letting him also Chain Attack twice in a Dragon Unit's combat as well? <br><br>

How this works is that when Other Half triggers a Chain Attack from Robin, it checks for Robin's Japanese Name (ルフレ) within the game's code to trigger the effect. Alfred's Japanese Name (ア<b>ルフレ</b>ッド) contains the same characters as Robin's Japanese Name. Thus the game will think Alfred is also Robin and extend this effect to him. If Alfred is reclassed into a Backup Class or has Lucina equipped, then whenever Alfred Chain Attacks, he will do so twice for a Dragon Unit Engaged with Chrom & Robin. This also applies if Alfred has the Dual Assist(+) skill equipped. <br><br>

This build is quite interesting and fun to play around with if you can set it up right with Alear and Alfred, you could have 4 Chain Attacks in single combat. That's 40% of the foe's HP potentially gone if they all land (which is more likely if Alear has Charm unlocked). <br><br>

For proof of this working, please refer to <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CfZ6_O7IE9Q&ab_channel=BigKlingy">this video by the YouTuber BigKlingy. </a> <br><br>

As of this profile's writing (December 2024), this has not been patched and it seems like it will never be patched. So enjoy it. 

</details>


# **Final Thoughts** <br>
If I had to describe Chrom & Robin in a single word it would be "Confused". As I've said before, Chrom & Robin spread themselves too thin by having tools in their kit that appeal to a variety of units. However, there are few Units/Classes that can benefit from their entire skill set. Physical Units get low returns from Other Half's Magic boost and two-thirds of their Engage Weapons while Magical Units don't benefit from Brute Force and will have weaker Giga Levin Sword Damage. Despite this, I lean more towards putting Chrom on Magical Units as that +10 MAG from Other Half goes a long way. Now that I think about it, their kit being all over the place reminds me of a couple who can't seem to agree on something... fitting considering the game isn't subtle about portraying them as a couple.
