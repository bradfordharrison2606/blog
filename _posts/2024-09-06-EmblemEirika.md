# **Introduction**
Eirika (& Ephraim) represents the 3rd and final GBA Fire Emblem Game: **The Sacred Stones**. They are an Emblem that is actually two Emblems in one, where each of the Azure Twins offer different abilities and benefits to their bearer. While they might seem complicated on paper, in practice, they are very similar to Roy. They are generalist Emblems designed to make anyone a better overall combat unit. However, Eirika (& Ephraim) have the added bonus of being excellent at exterminating Corrupted Foes.

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Eirika and Ephraim have a grand total of 5 Sync Skills but because of their unique mechanics, you can only use 3 of them at any given time. <br><br>

Eirika grants <b>Lunar Brace</b> and <b>Gentility</b> which makes her wearer hit harder and take less damage respectively. That extra damage from Lunar Brace might sound low in theory but in practice, by the time you have access to this Skill, most enemies will have enough Defense that the extra damage is very much worth it. Taking 3 less Damage due to Gentility adds a decent amount of bulk to Eirika's bearer. <br><br>

Meanwhile, Ephraim grants <b>Solar Brace</b> and <b>Bravery</b> to his bearer. Solar Brace is especially useful as the HP you recover scales based on the damage you deal to the enemy. This means that Glass Cannon characters get especially good mileage out of this as it lets them recover any HP they might have lost after taking a hit. Bravery adding a flat damage bonus is just icing on the cake. Ephraim is great for dealing with low Defense enemies like Magic Users as Lunar Brace won't add that much damage.<br><br>

While this might seem complicated, a good Rule of Thumb is that <b>Eirika works better with Physical Units</b> while <b>Ephrain is more suited to Magical Units</b>. Lunar Brace only activates with physical weapons and Gentility will cushion the blow of any counterattacks from the enemy. Meanwhile, Bravery can act a lot like Celica's Resonance where that extra 3 Damage can be enough to secure a 1-Round Kill, and Solar Brace can keep your Mages topped off on HP. You'll want to use Eirika and Ephraim on units that can reliably double enemies to maximize their respective effects. <br><br>

Let's circle back to the Azure Twins' first Sync Skill: <b>Night and Day</b>. This skill is both simple and complicated. This skill allows you to switch which Twin is in the lead and which set of Sync Skills you have equipped. <b>Night and Day DOES NOT use your turn</b>, so you can swap between the Twins to see which Skills are better for a given situation. However, the thing with Night and Day is that it does not only affect Eirika and Ephraim's bearer. This Sync Skill also affects ANYONE who has inherited Lunar/Solar Brace and/or Gentility/Bravery. Here is an example to show what I mean. <br><br>

Imagine you have 3 Units. Alear is wearing the Ring of the Azure Twins. Vander has inherited Lunar Brace, and Clanne has inherited Gentility. When Alear uses Night and Day to switch to Ephraim (Solar Brace & Bravery), Vander and Clanne will also switch to Solar Brace and Bravery respectively as well. This happens if you use Night and Day again to switch back to Eirika on a later turn. <br><br>

What this means is that anyone who has inherited these Sync Skills is at the mercy of whoever is wearing the Ring of the Azure Twins. So, if you're planning on spreading these Sync Skills around (good luck with that), do be mindful of that as some Units benefit from one of the Twins' Sync Skills but not the other. Lastly, if tyou enter a battle and do not deploy a Unit wearing the Ring of the Azure Twins, anyone who has inherited their Sync Skills will default to Eirika's Skills (Lunar Brace & Gentility). <br><br>

So you might think all of this is way too complicated with so many balls to juggle by switching between the Twins. Well then, why not both? This is where we come to Eirika and Ephraim's Engage Skill which gives you the best of both worlds.


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

When Engaged, Eirika and Ephraim grant one of the more unique Engage Skills in the game: <b>Sacred Twins</b>. What makes this Engage Skill stand out is that it doesn't grant any additional effects. Instead, it acts as if both Eirika and Ephraim are leading by merging their Sync Skills. Lunar Brace and Solar Brace combine to make <b>Eclipse Brace</b> while Gentility and Bravery combine to make <b>Blue Skies</b>. <br><br>

<b>Eclipse Brace</b> gives you the damage boost of Lunar Brace (assuming you're using a physical weapon) which increases the HP you recover from Solar Brace. <br><br>

<b>Blue Skies</b> is simpler but no less effective. For as long as it's active, you will ALWAYS inflict more damage and take less damage, regardless of what type of weapon you're wielding. <br><br>

It's no exaggeration that Sacred Twins makes The Azure Twins' Engage Form one of the stronger ones in the game. It turns almost anyone into a combat powerhouse for at least 3 Turns. One last thing is that while Engaged, anyone else who has inherited Eirika and Ephraim's Sync Skills will also have their those skills turned into Eclipse Brace and/or Blue Skies. <br><br>

But the fun doesn't stop there as the Twin's Engage Attack keeps the powerhouse train going.

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Twin Strike</b> might seem lacklustre at first glance as you're only doing two basic attacks, but remember that <b>Twin Strike will ALWAYS be boosted by the effects of Eclipse Brace and Blue Skies</b>. These effects apply to BOTH hits of Twin Strike which causes its damage to skyrocket to nuke status. However, if you use a magical weapon like the Levin Sword or one of Eirika's Engage Weapons, then only Blue Skies will trigger. <br><br>

But it doesn't stop there. Remember when I said the Azure Twins specialize in exterminating Corrupted? Twin Strike has the added benefit of dealing Effective Damage against Corrupted. This is amazing as by the time you have access to the Ring of the Azure Twins, Corrupted are much more common in Main Story Chapters. If you want to get the most mileage out of Twin Strike, especially against non-Corrupted Foes, use a Smash Weapon like a Steel or Silver Blade.<br><br>

I recommend saving Twin Strike for Corrupted Wyrm enemies as not only are they incredibly difficult to kill without Effectiveness Damage (Especially on Maddening Difficulty) but like all Engage Attacks, you don't have to risk eating a counterattack when you use Twin Strike. Even without an Anti-Corrupted weapon of your own (yet), Ephraim more than picks up the slack which we'll discuss in the following section. Alternatively, you could use Twin Strike to break a Boss' Revival Stone, especially if you're leveraging the Class Bonuses. <br><br>

The Class Bonuses are all about dealing more damage. Dragons boost the damage of both hits of Twin Strike. Cavalry get a ludicrous boost to the damage Ephraim deals. That 50% Extra Damage is overkill which is never a bad thing in Fire Emblem. After some play testing with Alear as both a Divine Dragon and Paladin, I can safely say that the Class Bonuses are roughly even in damage.


</details>

<summary> Explanation </summary>

Unlike everything else in their kit, the Engage Weapons exclusively come from Eirika. <br><br>

Eirika's <b>Rapier</b> has nearly identical stats to Marth's version, but lacks the +20 Avoid and is slightly heavier. It does have a 10% Critical Rate but that's hardly a fair trade-off. <br><br>

The <b>Wind Sword</b> is another example of good magical weapons in Engage. Unlike the Levin Sword or Leif's Light Brand, the Wind Sword is effective against Fliers. While this won't amount to much against Griffin Knights and their high Resistance Stats, this can score you some kills against Wyvern Knights who usually have low Resistance Stats. <br><br>

<b>Siegmund</b> is Ephraim's Engage Weapon and it functions similarly to Leif's Master Bow. Siegmund is exclusive to Twin Strike. However, unlike the Master Bow, Siegmund has fantastic stats across board and Corrupted Effectiveness. Siegmund is the reason Twin Strike is effectively a <b>"Delete One Corrupted Wyrm Button"</b> as it will ALWAYS be used for Twin Strike and have 42 Might against Corrupted enemies.

<summary> Explanation </summary>

Eirika's (and Ephraim's) <b>Sacred Engrave</b> is a <b>High Risk, High Reward</b> Engraving as it boosts Critical Rate and Hit Rate but at the cost of making you more susceptible to enemy Critical Hits and less evasive. This Engrave has the following use cases. <br>

<ul>
<li>If you have the <b>Olwen S Bond Ring which grants Dire Thunder (Thunder Tomes become Brave Weapons)</b>, then you can put this Engrave on a Thunder Tome to not only boost the tome's accuracy to a staggering 120 but noticeably increase its Critical Rate.</li> 

<li>You can put this Engrave on Units who are bulky enough to survive Enemy Critical Hits like <b>Generals, Great Knights, or Timerra's Picket Class</b>. Alternatively, if you're using the <b>Vantage+Wrath Combo with the Ring of the Radiant Hero</b>, then this Engrave is a good choice as that Emblem Ring makes its bearer incredibly durable and tanky while completely disregarding their Avoid stat.</li> 
</ul>

Additionally, if you are using Weapon Engraves that have a Dodge penalty, such as the Sacred Engrave, these Skills could be a way to minimize their impact.</li><br>

<li>Their Weapon Skill is <b>Lance Agility</b>. Like the other Agility Skills, I'm not too fond of this. However, it can see some niche use on Lance Dodge Tanks when combined with Weapon Engraves that boost Avoid. </li><br>

<li>Lastly, Eirika and Ephraim provide <b>Sword Proficiency</b> and <b>Lance Proficiency</b> respectively. Without going too deep into spoilers, Ephraim's Lance Proficiency might be especially welcome by the time you obtain the Ring of the Azure Twins.</li> 

</ul>

Unlike other Emblems, Eirika and Ephraim are an example of their Engage Skill getting stronger as you increase their Bond Level. This isn't reflected in the in-game descriptions, but I like to refer to their upgraded Engage Skill as <b>Sacred Twins+</b>. <br><br>

What this means is that Eclipse Brace and Blue Skies also get upgraded to their + versions, combining the effects of the + versions of their respective set of Sync Skills. This means that the Azure Twins' Engage Form becomes an even bigger powerhouse. <br><br>

However, one caveat is that Sacred Twins+ only gives you Eclipse Brace+ and Blue Skies+ if you've unlocked Lunar/Solar Brace+and Gentility/Bravery+ respectively. <br><br>

What this means in practice is that, say for example, you're at Bond 16 with Eirika and Ephraim and activate their Engage. This also applies to other units that have inherited the Brace Skills and Gentility/Bravery. If they only have the base versions and not the + versions, Sacred Twins+ will only turn them into Eclipse Brace and Blue Skies. <br><br>

<b>In short, Bond 18 is where you'll get the most mileage out of Sacred Twins+</b>

# **Character Builds**
I compared the Azure Twins to Roy as they are both generalist Emblems that don't require specific builds. Eirika and Ephraim work well on a wide variety of units due to their Engage Form and Sacred Twins(+) giving their bearer a large boost to their power. I find myself leaning towards Physical Builds that leverage Eirika's abilities but there's nothing stopping you from trying Ephraim out on dedicated Mages.



<details>
<summary> Twin (Nuclear) Strike</summary>

This build leverages the +50% Damage Bonus Cavalry Units get from Ephraim's half of Twin Strike. This turns an already powerful Engage Attack into a Nuke. <b>Cavalry Classes like Paladin, Great Knights, Mage Knights, and Wolf Knights</b> are viable candidates for this build. Wolf Knights especially benefit as they have high Speed Stats which allow them to reliably double enemies and get increased mileage out of the Azure Twins' Sync Skills. <br><br>

If you decide to go this route, please ensure that your Cavalry Unit can Swords to maximize the damage from your half of Twin Strike with Smash Weapons as even a Steel Blade has higher Might than Sieglinde when attacking non-Corrupted enemies. 

</details>

<details>
<summary> Fist of the Lunar Star</summary>

While Qi Adept Classes don't get any bonuses from Twin Strike, they still make great partners for the Azure Twins. Recall how I said that to get the most mileage out of the Azure Twins' Sync Skills, it's best to use them on units who can reliably double attack enemies. Qi Adepts use Body Arts as their primary weapon and most Arts Weapons have the Brave Effect where they strike twice per normal attack when initiating combat. <br><br>

<b>Martial Masters</b> have decent Speed Stats, meaning they will strike 4 times if they initiate combat and all 4 of those hits will be boosted by the Brace Skills and Bravery/Blue Skies. Martial Masters are mainly a Support Class through their healing and Breaking Tome Users but the Azure Twins can turn them into offensive powerhouses. 


</details>


# **Final Thoughts** <br>

Despite their complexity of being two Emblems for the price of one, Eirika and Ephraim can be boiled down to "<b>Do more Damage. Take less Damage</b>" and this mentality will get you pretty far with them. Their Anti-Corrupted abilities are a nice cherry on top of their abilities. If there is an enemy (especially a Corrupted Enemy) that makes you think "Ok this guy needs to die RIGHT NOW!", sic Eirika and Ephraim on them and watch them vanish from the map.
