# **Introduction**
Soren is a magic-focused Emblem in a similar vein to Celica. He designed to boost the raw firepower of dedicated mages. However, where Soren differentiates himself is that he (fittingly) leans more towards a strategic role in battle inflicting debuffs and exploiting the enemies' weaknesses. Unfortunately, Soren is a bit hyper-specialized as the vast majority of his kit only benefits Tome users.

<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Assign Decoy</b> can be useful if you want the enemy to target one specific unit in your army. If you're using the Ike Vantage+Wrath Build, then this can force enemies to target them and subsequently die to a Critical Hit. However, if you combine this with Lyn's Call Doubles, you could have a good buffer against enemy attacks. As explained in <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/Emblems/Lyn#engage-skill">Lyn's Profile</a>, the Enemy AI prioritizes Lyn's Doubles, and when they're all dead, whoever you used Assign Decoy on will be their next target. <br><br>

<b>Anima Focus</b> is where Soren begins to show his role as a debuffer. This encourages Soren's wielder to run Tomes of the 3 standard types of magic. I prefer the effects granted by Fire and Wind tomes as the penalties they inflict could be helpful in either killing or slowing down the target. <br><br>

<b>Keen Insight</b> (and its upgraded form) focuses on exploiting the weaknesses of enemies via Effectiveness Damage. The damage bonus is flat +5/7, meaning that it is added on after regular damage calculations. With Soren himself, this skill mainly applies when his bearer uses Wind Magic against Flying Units. <br><br>

<b>Block Recovery</b> is Soren's final Sync Skill and it's an interesting one. In Engage, Break only lasts for one combat. If you attack a Broken Foe, they will regain their weapon afterward. Block Recovery is one of the few ways outside the Fracture Staff that the Break status can persist through multiple combats. So, if you're facing a tough and bulky enemy like Bosses with multiple HP Bars, this can let you beat them up without fear of retaliation. However, on Maddening Difficulty, the majority of Bosses are immune to Break, this skill is useless on them.  The other thing working against this skill is the activation rate. Without factoring in reclassing, Mages and other Tome-wielding classes aren't exactly known for their high-speed stats, so don't expect to see this skill activating a lot. Block Recovery is in the camp of "It's nice when it happens, but don't rely on it."

</details>
 
# Engage Skill

| ![Pic]({{ site.baseurl }}/img/soren/FE17_Flare_Icon.webp) <br> **Flare** |When attacking with Tomes, inflicts RES-20% on Foe, and Unit recovers 50% of Damage dealt. |

|Dragon| Critical Rate is Doubled |
|Mystical| Inflicts extra RES-10% on Foe (Total -30% RES) |
|Qi Adept| Recover 100% Damage Dealt to Foe |

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Flare</b> is a reference to the Occult Mastery Skill of Sages and Bishops in Path of Radiance and works similarly by decreasing the Resistance of the Foe and recovering HP. Not only does this increase the damage of Soren's Users while Engaged but also keeps them topped up on HP which adds a nice bit of bulk to normally fragile magic users. <br><br>

The Class Bonuses are straightforward. Mystical Units increase the Resistance penalty inflicted to 30% while Qi Adepts recover HP equal to the damage they deal. <br><br> 

The Dragon Bonus is where things get interesting as they double their Critical Rate while Engaged. Now this might seem useless on Alear as neither Dragon Child nor Divine Dragon can use Tomes naturally. However, without spoiling too much of Engage's story, there is another playable Dragon Unit in the base game who happens to use Tomes naturally and this 2nd Dragon Unit is arguably Soren's best user. We'll discuss this later in the Character Builds Section.

</details>

# Engage Attack

| ![Pic]({{ site.baseurl }}/img/soren/FE17_Cataclysm_Icon.webp) <br> **Cataclysm**  | Use to attack foes in an area with Fire, Thunder and Wind magic at 40% damage each. <br> Wind is Effective: Flying. |

|**Unit Type**|**Bonus**|
|Dragon| Sets Terrain on Fire afterwards. |
|Mystical |+10% Damage |
|Qi Adept | 20% Chance of Break |

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Soren's <b>Cataclysm</b> is an AoE-style Magic attack that attacks a plus-shaped area (for a maximum of 5 enemies) using the 3 standard types of magic. Since we're using Fire, Thunder, and Wind, Cataclysm will inflict every debuff from Anima Focus (assuming you're at Bond 4 or higher). Since Wind is Effective against Fliers, it will receive the damage boost from Keen Insight(+). <br><br> 

If an ally wearing Ike's ring is adjacent to Soren's user, then it becomes Cataclysm+ which increases the range to a 3x3 Square area for a maximum of 9 enemies. If you have a bunch of low Resistance enemies like Generals or Great Knights clustered together, Cataclysm can be a decent way to soften them up. Below is a diagram comparing the two versions of this attack.<br><br>

The Class Bonuses are pretty straightforward. Dragons set the affected area on Fire which is always nice for slowing down enemies. Mystical Units deal more damage, and Qi Adepts get a small chance at Break which isn't anything to write home about.

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Bolting</b> should be familiar to any Fire Emblem fan. It's a long-range siege tome, allowing Soren's user to strike from far away. It's one of the few ways outside of Lyn's Astra Storm to attack from extreme distances. Its stats are awful and since it's a Thunder Tome, it cannot double but Bolting is more for utility than damage. You could use this to pick off weak enemies from a distance or use it as a Mini Astra Storm to aggro Bosses into charging at you (See Lyn's Profile for more details). If you want to increase the accuracy of Bolting, try leveraging Support Bonuses or inherit Hit+ or Divine Pulse from Sigurd or Byleth. <br><br>

<b>Reflect</b> at Bond 10 is an oddity as it's a Staff like Celica's Recover. However, Reflect wasn't even in either of the Tellius Games. This staff is... weird to use right as it's meant to be used on low Resistance characters like Generals or Great Knights to get the maximum effect. Even then, that means exposing those units to their main weakness for a lot of damage and potentially dying if Reflect isn't enough of a damage buffer. However, Reflect has another purpose: <b>Grinding EXP and SP</b>. The staff can be used on allies, even if they're at full HP, and affects multiple allies in a single use which awards SP for each of them. <br><br>

Soren's final Engage Weapon is the SS Wind Tome from Radiant Dawn, <b>Rexcalibur</b>. It has solid stats across the board except for Weight which can potentially inflict Speed penalties on Mages with low Build stats. What stands out is this weapon's Accuracy. At 105 Accuracy, even characters with mediocre DEX stats will have near-perfect hit rates with this thing. Excluding Surge tomes which have infinite accuracy and thus cannot be miss, Rexcalibur might have the highest Accuracy of any weapon the player can use. <br><br>

<b>Cataclysm</b> is what the game uses to calculate the damage dealt by Soren's Engage Attack. It treats this weapon as a Fire, Thunder, and Wind Tome for each hit of the attack. If you've used Cataclysm and wonder why it's doing such mediocre damage, even when used by dedicated Mages, that 4 Might is the answer as it doesn't do much to compensate for the 40% Damage modifier of the Engage Attack. 

</details>

<summary> Explanation </summary>

Being the cutthroat, pragmatic mercenary strategist he is, Soren's Inheritance is not cheap. His skills are going to cost you a pretty SP penny. This isn't necessarily a bad thing as Soren's Inheritance is hyper-specialised towards Mages and Tome-users. <br><br>

Out of all Soren's inheritable Sync Skills, the only ones I recommend are <b>Anima Focus</b> and <b>Keen Insight</b>. Assign Decoy might be nice to inherit but more often than not, Soren's user having this skill is enough. <b>Block Recovery</b> is exclusive to Tome-wielding Classes and I already discussed why this makes it unreliable earlier. <br><br>

<b>Anima Focus</b> is relatively cheap for the effects it gives. If you like to debuff the enemy, then I would recommend having one or two of your dedicated Mages inherit this skill. <br><br>

<b>Keen Insight</b> is definitely the skill with the most universal appeal in Soren's kit. When you're facing enemy Fliers like Wyvern Knights or Griffin Knights, that +5 damage boost can be enough to one-shot them if you're using a Bow or Wind Magic. <br><br>

Soren's Stat Skill is <b>Magic/Resistance+</b>. We've seen from Micaiah that Resistance boosts are relatively cheap but since it's tied to a Magic boost, that drastically inflates the price tag of these skills. <br><br>

His Guard Skill is <b>Magic Guard</b> which decreases damage from enemy Tome attacks. Like Bow Guard with Fliers, I wouldn't recommend putting on Armored Units like Generals because even -5 Damage won't be enough to save them from taking lots of damage. However, it might be enough of a buffer to prevent the unit from dying to an enemy Mage. <br><br>

Lastly, Soren gives 3 Weapon Proficiencies: <b>Knives, Staves, and Tomes</b>. Like Micaiah, Knife Proficiency is a reference to the Tellius Games, specifically Path of Radiance where Mages, upon promotion to Sage, had the option to wield Staves or Knives as a second weapon type... everyone normally chose Staves because Knives were terrible in Path of Radiance. Tome and Staff Proficiency being locked at Bonds 12 and 16 respectively is perfectly in character for Soren. He's going to make you work for those Proficiencies. <br><br>

Fun Fact: Soren gives out the exact same Proficiencies as Micaiah.

# **Character Builds**

<details>
<summary> Flare Dragon Slayer </summary>

This is a build I am the most familiar with and quite frankly, it is the only Soren Build you will ever need. <br><br>

Recall earlier in this profile I mentioned that in Engage, there is another playable Dragon Unit aside from Alear who specializes in Tomes and is widely considered Soren's best user. For the sake of avoiding spoilers, we'll call this Dragon character <b>Levey</b>. To make this build for Levey, you'll need the following:<br><br>

* Levey must remain in their default class which makes them a Dragon Unit and allows them to use Tomes naturally. <br><br>

* Their personal Tome (which has a default 10% Critical Rate) with an Engrave that boosts Critical Rate (<b>Lyn, Eirika, Corrin, Camilla, The 13th Emblem</b>). The +20/30 Critical Bonus becomes +40/60 when Flare is active. This can work with Elsurge but I don't recommend this as Surge Tomes are locked to 1 Range, meaning Levey can't counter ranged enemies. <br><br>

* Levey inherits <b>Hold Out</b> from Roy and <b>Vantage</b> from Leif. This is the easiest part as 1) by the time Levey joins, you have both Roy and Leif and 2) Levey's starting SP when they join your army is 2500 which is exactly enough to inherit Hold Out (2000 SP) and Vantage (500 SP) without the need for any Skill Books. <br><br>

This build is effectively the Magical equivalent to Ike's Vantage+Wrath Combo which is fitting for Soren. Flare on Levey doubles their Critical Rate and Levey hitting like a truck with magical attacks thank to Flare's base effect. This will make sure any enemy they attack will die horribly to a Critical Hit. With this Build Levey's Critical Rate can reach absolutely ludicrous numbers in the 80s and 90s and the following image is proof.<br><br>

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/img/soren/flarecrits.JPEG" alt="Description of image"> <br><br>

Hold Out and Vantage are there as insurance policies. Flare restores HP upon dealing damage, which will keep Levey above the threshold for Hold Out to activate. Furthermore, Hold Out will guarantee Levey survives the current battle with 1 HP. This is where Vantage comes in if Levey is at 1 HP when an enemy attacks, Vantage will trigger, make Levey attack first, and most likely land a Critical Hit to not only annihilate the enemy but restore a good chunk of their HP. Wash, Rinse, and Repeat for 3-4 Turns. <br><br>

I cannot stress how overpowered this build is. This build turns Levey into an Enemy-Phase juggernaut that can wipe out entire squads of enemy units. If you're playing Maddening and have no problem using DLC, <b>I HIGHLY RECOMMEND</b> this build as it makes the final few Main Story Chapters on Maddening much more bearable. <br><br>

Unfortunately, like every other build in Engage, this Soren build has problems. Enemy Chain Attacks (These ignore Hold Out), 3-Ranged Bows and Spells (Enemies will be out of Levey's counterattack range) and Arts weapons (Which break Tome Users) are the main weaknesses of these builds so be mindful of them. 


</details>


# **Final Thoughts** <br>
Overall, Soren is hyperspecialized towards Mages and Tome-users which limits the kinds of units he synergizes with. This isn't necessarily a bad thing as it means less competition for who gets to wear his Bracelet. Soren offers a good amount of debuffing and debilitating abilities (though, he's no Corrin) along with being one of the few ways of attacking at long-range outside of Lyn. Soren truly shines when equipped on a very specific Dragon Unit that can leverage all of his Magic and Tome-specific abilities to become a juggernaut that annihilates everything that dares oppose them.  Whatever you decide to use Soren for, he'll be there to advise you with wise counsel and acumen.
