# **Introduction**
Celica represents the 2nd Fire Emblem Game: Gaiden and its 3DS Remake, Echoes. She is the first purely Magic-focused Emblem the Player acquires and is one of the only Magic-focused Emblems in the game. She specializes in increasing the raw magical damage of her wearer, providing tools to counter Corrupted Foes, and giving the occasional burst of extreme mobility.

That increase to Strength might seem out of place, but there are a few classes in Engage that are Mixed Attackers who can switch between Physical and Magical attacks. These include Mage Knights, Griffin Knights, and the special Vidame Class.

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Holy Stance</b> as a Sync Skill isn't all that impressive (for now) as dealing 10% Damage back to the Foe usually results in single-digit damage. It only works during the Enemy Phase and since Mages are normally quite squishy, you don't want to leave them exposed to enemy attacks for this skill to even trigger. But don't worry, Celica has much better ways of dealing with Corrupted Foes. <br><br>

<b>Resonance</b> is where Celica begins to shine as a Magic-focused Emblem. That +2 Damage might not seem all that powerful, but oftentimes in Engage, Mages have trouble doubling non-Armoured foes, and that extra damage could be enough to finish a foe. However be warned, while that -1 HP cost can't kill Celica's users, it can put them in danger of dying to an enemy attack, so be careful when using it. <br><br>

<b>Favorite Food</b> is a skill I've found rather niche. It can be used for a gimmicky build with a certain character, but that build is often just for laughs and giggles. However, if you aren't throwing your Packed Lunches into the Well, this skill can be used for back-to-back Celica Engages during a chapter. Or it can be used with other Emblems if it is inherited.


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Echo</b> is an Engage Skill I undervalued at first, but I've come to see the value in its ability to split your fire between two targets. Mystical Units get the best deal from Echo as that extra 10% Damagae for each Echo Attack effectively makes it a 20% Damage boost if you are attacking the same target. <br><br>

One application of Echo is using it to finish off a weakened enemy before using the second Echo Attack to soften up another enemy. Furthermore, if you're running a Tome with an Engrave that boosts Critical Rate and you're doubling the enemy, that's effectively 4 chances to score a Critical Hit. <br><br>

Lastly, Echo is very useful for stacking Chain Attacks. Each attack of Echo counts as a separate Combat, meaning your Backup Units who Chain Attacked in the first Echo, will do so again in the 2nd Combat. 

</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

How you use <b>Warp Ragnarok</b> (WR) depends on whether you're in the Early or Late Game. In the Early Game, WR is basically the magical version of Marth's Lodestar Rush as another "Delete One Enemy" Button. Regular enemies won't have the RES or sheer bulk to survive a hit from WR. In the Late Game, WR usually won't one-shot an enemy, even if Celica's user has a high Magic Stat. Despite this, WR still has its uses as a mobility tool. The ability to travel at most 15 spaces is pretty handy at times. <br><br>

As for the class bonuses, Dragons getting 3 Range is nice and the Mystical Bonus of adding raw damage can help with killing enemies. The Cavalry and Mystical Bonuses are interesting. For Cavalry Units, Mage Knights are great candidates since it's a good class in Engage and the best candidates for this class have decent Magic Stats. As for Flying Units, there are only 4 Flying Tome using Classes in Engage, and they are all locked to specific characters. However, Griffin Knights can get in on the fun. While they don't use Tomes, they still have pretty decent Magic Stats. 

</details>

<b>Seraphim</b> is Celica's best Engage Weapon bar none. While that 6 Might may look disappointing, remember this weapon is effective against Corrupted Foes. In Story Chapters, Corrupted Foes make up a huge chunk of the enemies, especially in the Late Game. When fighting Corrupted enemies, Seraphim's Might is tripled to 18, which is higher than most Bond 15 Engage Weapons. Outside of its effectiveness bonus, it is also accurate, and fairly light which makes it easier for Mages to double attack with it. <br><br>

<b>Recover</b> isn't anything too special. While it's nice to have a power Staff in an emergency, I rarely ever find myself using this Engage Weapon. Celica is better off spending her turns attacking rather than healing. If you need an Emblem with healing abilities, there is another Emblem who is MUCH better at healing than Celica.<br><br>

Last but not least we have <b>Ragnarok</b>. When you first obtain Celica, you can't use Ragnarok as a normal Engage Weapon like Seraphim. It can only be used during Celica's Engage Attack. <details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>
Celica's Echoes Engrave is purely defensive. That +50 Dodge makes whatever weapon you engrave with this into an 
"Anti-Critical" weapon. If an enemy has a Killer Weapon, then equipping a weapon with the Echoes Engrave can shut down any chance they have of scoring a Critical Hit on you. <br><br>

Another application of the Echoes Engrave is "Weight Fixing". A weapon's Weight in Engage is very important as if the Weight exceeds a character's Build (BLD), they will incur a penalty to their Speed Stat based on the difference. Reducing the Weight of a weapon, even by 1 point, can mean doubling an enemy during battle. <br><br>

If you're trying to use heavier weapons like Steel or Silver weapons, the Levin Sword, or Tomes like Bolganone, put Celica's Engrave on that weapon. The -1 Might penalty is not a big deal compared to the -1 Weight bonus. It's a fair tradeoff when your Units lack the raw Build to double with these weapons naturally. 


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Celica's Skill Inheritance focuses on boosting the raw Magical abilities of Units. This means dedicated Mages get the most value from Celica. Unfortunately, actually inheriting Celica's skills may prove difficult as Celica's more impactful Skills are stupidly expensive. <br><br>

<b>Resonance</b> is useful for Mages as it boosts their damage but that 2000 SP price tag is a deal breaker. This is a trend in Engage. Any Inheritable Skill that boosts damage, either directly or indirectly via boosting your raw Stats (Strength or Magic) will cost you a pretty SP penny. <br><br>

Despite being dirt cheap, <b>Holy Stance</b> isn't worth inheriting on most Units. Only bouncing back 10% of the Damage taken isn't anything to write home about. However, that might change later on. <br><br>

Fittingly for a Magic-focused Emblem, Celica's Stat Skill is <b>Magic+</b>, and just like Resonance is expensive. Often times I don't find myself inheriting these skills as dedicated Mages in Engage are designed to kill Armored Units on their natural Magic stat alone. Armored Units have such low Resistance stats that the extra Magic would be overkill. <br><br>

<b>Tome Precision</b> is Celica's Weapon Skill and I find this more useful than Magic+. These skills can be useful on Mages with accuracy issues, especially when using less accurate Tomes like Thunder, Elthunder, or Thoron. They are reasonably priced as well. <br><br>

<b>Favorite Food</b> may be worth putting on someone if you enjoy using a particular Emblem on that specific Unit. For example, if you plan on using Marth's Mercurius for grinding Levels, having back-to-back Engages lets Marth's wielder rack up the EXP fast. <br><br>

Last but not least are Celica's Weapon Proficiencies. She gives a very useful combination. <b>Sword Proficiency and Tome Proficiency</b> are required to Reclass into the Mage Knight Class. As I've discussed before, Mage Knight is one of the better classes in Engage for its Mixed Attacker capabilities. Sword Proficiency isn't anything special as we can get this from Marth and many other Emblems. Tome Proficiency however is special as Celica is one of only two Emblems in the Base Game to grant this and much earlier than the other Emblem, making her your go-to if you want Tome Proficiency on someone.

</details>

# **Character Builds**

To be honest, out of all the Base Game Emblems, Celica is the one I have the least practical experience with. As I've implied throughout this profile, Celica works best on dedicated offensive Mages.

<details>
<summary> Mystical Echo Echo Echo</summary>

If you haven't figured it out by now, Celica works best on dedicated Mages, especially Mystical Units due to the bonuses they get from Echo and Warp Ragnarok. You can't go wrong by putting Celica on Units like <b>Sages, High Priests</b>, and the special <b>Vidame</b> Class. This build is basic but practical.

</details>

<details>
<summary> Trusty Magical Steed </summary>

<b>Mage Knights, Griffin Knights</b>, and the special <b>Lindwurm</b> Class are the go-to candidates for this build. Mage Knights and Lindwurms use Tomes and thus benefit from Resonance and Echo. The extra movement bonus from Warp Ragnarok is a nice bonus. Griffin Knights get the shorter end of the stick as they can't use Tomes and thus can't benefit from Resonance and are forced to use Celica's Engage Weapons for Echo. However, they can still wield magic weapons like the Levin Sword and Flame Lance to benefit from the Magic boost Celica's Ring provides.

</details>


# **Final Thoughts** <br>
Celica is an Emblem I’ve found myself comparing to Marth as another "Training Wheels" Emblem. In the early game, she is all about letting you teleport around the battlefield and incinerate enemies with Warp Ragnarok. This makes clearing maps much easier when your resources are limited. By the end game, however, I find that Celica drops off a little, though not nearly as much as Marth. By that point, Celica isn't a hotly contested Emblem so I put her either on a Mage who doesn't have a more dedicated Emblem, or someone who could benefit from Warp Ragnarok's mobility. Despite all of this, Celica is a perfectly fine Emblem to use.
