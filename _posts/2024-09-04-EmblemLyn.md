# **Introduction**
Lyn represents the 2nd of the GBA Fire Emblems and the 1st Fire Emblem to be released outside of Japan: **Blazing Sword**. Lyn's gameplay is focused on 2 things: **Archery** and **Speed**. She is designed to boost her wearer's Speed to absolutely insane heights to out-speed the enemy. This is what makes her one of the best Combat-focused Emblems in the eyes of many players.

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Lyn only has 2 Sync Skills, like Roy. However, just like Roy, Lyn doesn't need a 3rd one as her two Sync Skills are designed to work hand-in-hand with each other. <br><br>

<b>Alacrity</b> is Lyn's primary Sync Skill and the key to her entire kit and playstyle. In modern Fire Emblem Games, during combat, if a Unit's Speed Stat exceeds that of an enemy's Speed Stat by at least 5, they will perform an additional attack. This applies to both you and the enemy. The flow of combat when you are doubling an enemy is as follows. <br>
<ul>
	<li>Your 1st Attack -> Enemy Counterattack -> Your 2nd Attack</li>
</ul>
Alacrity changes up this order by letting you get both of your attacks off <b>BEFORE</b> the enemy can counterattack. When Alacrity triggers, the flow of combat becomes the following. <br>
<ul>
	<li>Your 1st Attack -> Your 2nd Attack -> Enemy Counterattack</li>
</ul>
		
What this means, is that Alacrity gives Lyn's user the Brave Effect of allowing them to attack twice in a row before the enemy counterattacks. What's even better, is that Alacrity stacks with Brave Weapons, allowing you to get <b>FOUR (4)</b> attacks before the enemy can counter. Alacrity lets you take out dangerous enemies before they have a chance to hurt you. A word of caution though. Alacrity cannot trigger if you are using weapons that cannot follow up like Smash Weapons or Thunder Tomes. <br><br>

That 9-Speed activation threshold might look intimidating, but Lyn gives you numerous tools that make that threshold almost laughably easy to hit. One of those tools is her 2nd Sync Skill. <br><br>

<b>Speedtaker</b> allows you to "steal" Speed from defeated foes. This skill alone makes Lyn's wearer snowball like crazy because it feeds directly into Alacrity. Get a few kills with Speedtaker equipped and that 9-speed threshold for Alacrity is incredibly easy to hit. Alacrity makes it easier to kill opponents, which activates Speedtaker for even more Speed. Wash, Rinse, and Repeat until you have a lightning-fast specter of death roaming the battlefield.


</details>
 




## Engage Skill

| ![Divine Speed Pic]({{ site.baseurl }}/img/lyn/FE17_Call_Doubles_Icon.webp) <br> **Call Doubles** | Creates four (4) Illusory Doubles that can make Chain Attacks with unit. <br> Doubles have 1HP, a Mani Katti and identical stats as Unit. Disappear when Engage ends. |

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Call Doubles</b> is an interesting Engage Skill as it has a myriad of uses. The simplest use case is the Chain Attack potential. Having 4 allies who can Chain Attack as long as they are on the map, lets you rack up a lot of damage on enemies. Assuming all 4 Doubles land their attacks, the enemy has effectively lost 40% of the Max HP before you even attack them. The only downside is that Lyn's Phantoms will only Chain Attack for her wearer and no one else. <br><br>

Call Doubles has another use that is arguably more useful: <b>ENEMY AI MANIPULATION</b>. For the uninitiated, the Enemy AI in Fire Emblem Games loves to kill things. If you've played enough of the games, you would have noticed that Enemies will target any of your allied units they have a good chance of killing. So when the Enemy AI sees a 1 HP Double, it starts salivating as it sees something it is almost guaranteed to kill with a single hit. Thus, the enemy will prioritize killing the Double over attacking your other Units. I am not exaggerating when I say this quirk of the Enemy AI can <b>SAVE LIVES</b> if used correctly. If you have an ally that you believe the enemy will target and kill, have Lyn call up some Doubles to take the hit instead. Alternatively, you could place one of Lyn's Doubles in range of a dangerous enemy to lure them into attack range. There is no downside to sacrificing a Double as they are 100% disposable and will disappear when Lyn's Engage wears off anyway. <br><br>

The Class Bonuses are pretty decent. Dragons get 5 Doubles for more Chain Attack damage and potential lures. Flying Units make their Doubles slightly better dodge tanks. Even though Covert Units don't get a Class Bonus, the Doubles created are Covert Units themselves and thus still benefit from the doubled Avoid bonus they receive from favorable terrain. So, if you have a Covert Unit in cover use Call Doubles, those Doubles will be hard to hit, making them perfect distractions for the enemy who will be hellbent on trying to kill them. <br><br>

Despite Call Doubles having so many uses, be warned of its downsides. Call Doubles uses your action for that turn which means Lyn's user cannot attack after summoning them. Enemies killed by Lyn's Doubles do not award experience to anyone, not even Lyn's user. So be careful that they don't steal valuable experience from your units. <br><br>

Fun Fact: This Engage Skill is a callback to Lyn's Critical Animations back in her home game where she would produce afterimage clones of herself before striking the enemy.

</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Astra Storm continues Lyn's stellar track record as an amazing Emblem. First things first, each hit of Astra Storm is subject to the "Rush Effect", meaning they can potentially do more damage than advertised. Besides being one of the very few ways the Player can attack enemies at long range, Astra Storm has a plethora of applications.
<ul>
<li><b>Deleting one Flying Enemy</b>. Griffin Knights and Wyvern Knights are some of the toughest enemies to kill. Even if you exploit their weakness to Bows, that isn't the silver bullet as not only are they fast, making them difficult to double, let alone hit them and they are bulky, meaning they can survive a Bow attack. Astra Storm lets you ignore these two hurdles as 1) Engage Attacks cannot miss and 2) Astra Storm will always do bonus damage because it requires a Bow to use. </li><br>

<li><b>Aggroing Bosses</b>. In Engage, many Chapters only require you to kill the boss to win. Usually, the Boss is on the other side of the map from your starting position and they remain stationary. This means you have to fight your way across the map to get to them. Astra Storm lets you ignore that as once a Boss is attacked, they will immediately begin charging right at you. This means you can sit back and let the Boss come to you. This makes a large number of Chapters and Paralogues in Engage, much less of a headache to deal with, especially on Maddening Difficulty. If you use Astra Storm on Turn 1, that saves you a lot of work in the long run.</li>
</ul>

Unfortunately, since there are now "Smash Weapons" for Bows, there is no quick and easy way to pump up Astra Storm's damage. You either need to use a heavily forged Bow or wait until you've completed Lyn's Paralogue. 


</details>

If you're running the <b>Vantage-Wrath Combo</b> using the Ring of the Radiant Hero, then I highly recommend putting Lyn's Engrave on whatever Killer Weapon you're using with that build.

Lyn's Skill Inheritance is reasonably affordable across the board, especially for the value they give you. <br>

<ul>

<li><b>Alacrity</b> is decently priced for what it gives you. However, I only recommend inheriting on high Speed characters as hitting that 9 Speed threshold is a tall order without assistance from Lyn herself or her other inheritance.</li><br>

<li><b>Speed+</b> is Lyn's Stat Skill and it is by far the one that will give you the biggest bang for your SP buck. Even at the lowest tier, you'd be surprised how much of a different +1 Speed makes with Doubling Thresholds. Like Sigurd's Canter, Speed+ is almost universally useful. If you have a unit with an empty skill slot you can't decide what to do with, have them inherit a Speed+ Skill from Lyn.</li><br>

<li>By contrast, Lyn's Weapon Skill, <b>Bow Focus</b> is borderline useless. Bows in Engage have high Hit rates (For reference, the Longbow has the lowest Hit rate at 70%) and Bow-focused classes have high Dexterity Stats so they rarely have accuracy problems.</li><br>

<li>Lyn gives <b>Sword</b> and <b>Bow Proficiency</b>. These are nice to have but on their own, they don't lead to any powerhouse classes unless combined with other Weapon Proficiencies.</li><br>

<li>I saved <b>Speedtaker</b> for last because it's the most complicated to talk about. For complicated reasons that I will do my best to summarize, Speedtaker gets worse the more units you have it equipped on besides Lyn's main bearer. What happens is that more units with Speedtaker, means more competition to secure kills to stack Speed boosts. For every unit that inherits Speedtaker, that's an extra 5 enemies that they need to kill to reap the full benefits. Many Engage Players recommend passing Speedtaker on at most, 1 other Unit. If you're doing this, I recommend inheriting Alacrity as well as these two skills are designed to complement and work together.</li> 

</ul>
</details>

# **Character Builds**




<details>
<summary> Tactical Astra Storm Incoming!</summary>

If you want to maximize the range of Astra Storm to the full 20 Spaces, then consider pairing Lyn with <b>Covert Units</b>. With this build, not only does your range cover nearly the entire map, but it is much easier to aggro bosses on Turn 1 and make them charge at you. <br><br>

Unfortunately, this Build has some limitations, mainly the lack of Covert Classes. In Engage, there are only 5 Covert Classes, <b>Thieves, Archers, Snipers</b>, and 2 Character-specific Classes we'll discuss in a minute. Archers and Snipers get especially good mileage from Lyn as they can use their own, forged Bows with Lyn's Astra Storms. Thieves, while they are restricted to Lyn's Killer Bow or Mulagir for Astra Storm, can leverage the sheer Speed Lyn provides to make them even better Dodge Tanks. 

</details>

<details>
<summary> The Brodian Prince who isn't Diamant.</summary>

This Build is related to the previous one. There are 2 Cover Classes in Engage that are exclusive to the Character <b>Alcryst</b> (<b>Lord</b> and <b>Tireur d'elite</b>). Alcryst is commonly agreed to be one of Lyn's best users. Not only does he specialize in Bows, but his Class Skill once promoted is <b>Luna</b> (A DEX% chance to ignore half of the Foe's DEF/RES when attacking). Luna is subject to the "Rush Effect" where each hit of Astra Storm can proc the skill, leading to one-round kills, even on non-Flier targets. 


</details>


<details>
<summary> Wrecking Ball of Sacae</summary>

In Engage, many characters can be described as "Mighty Glaciers" where they hit hard but don't have the Speed to hit more than once per combat. This is where Lyn comes in with her sheer Speed. Lyn can turn Mighty Glaciers into Lightning Bruisers who hit hard AND fast. <br><br>

<b>Warriors</b> especially benefit from this Build as while their Bow Rank isn't that impressive (The highest rank they can go is B Rank which requires a Blue Proficiency in Bows), they don't need to wield anything stronger than a Steel Bow as the high Strength of the Warrior Class combined with Lyn's Speed is more than enough to get the job done. That's not even factoring in Mulagir once you unlock it. 

</details>

<details>
<summary> Radiant Shooting Star</summary>

This is more of a gimmicky Build for fun. The <b>Radiant Bow</b> is a magical bow that boasts an impressive base Might of 19 which triples to 57 against Flying Enemies. There are numerous characters in Engage with impressive Magic Stats and/or Growths, even when outside of dedicated Mage Classes. This build involves reclassing a Magic-focused Unit into Sniper, giving them a forged (and possibly engraved) Radiant Bow, and putting Lyn on them. This build is a surprisingly good boss killer, especially against enemies with low Resistance Stats. 

</details>


# **Final Thoughts** <br>

As the first Lord Western Fire Emblems would have met and one of the most popular Lords in the franchise, Lyn has definitely received the VIP treatment compared to her mediocre performance in her home game. It's like Night and Day. When the Devs asked Lyn how much Speed she wanted in her kit, she replied "Yes". <br>

She is a fantastic combat Emblem who can turn almost anyone into a One-Round Killing Machine while providing support and utility through her Skill Inheritance. Lyn is an Emblem you should not sleep on. <br>

Familiarize yourself with the ins and outs of her kit and you'll shoot down enemies like fish in a barrel without taking a scratch. Her only real weakness is that there's only one of her in the game, and no, Doubles don't count.
