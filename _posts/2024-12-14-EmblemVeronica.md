# **Introduction**
Veronica doesn't represent any mainline Fire Emblem Game. She represents the spinoff Mobile Game: <b>Fire Emblem Heroes</b>. Veronica can be summed in one single word: "Broken". I make no exaggeration when I say Veronica is one of the most powerful Emblems in the game and the only one who is locked behind a DLC paywall is completely justified. Every single piece of her kit is designed to break the game in one way or another.

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Book of Worlds</b> is Veronica's signature Sync Skill and it is quite the complicated one. How it works is that whenever you use the "Wait" command directly from the menu, BoW advances by one stage or "Book". The real kicker is that all these effects are cumulative. This means that every stage of this skill includes the effects of every stage preceding it. For example, if you attack while BoW is at Book 3: Death not only will you deal 10 Damage to the foe after combat, but it will also have the effects of Book 1: Seal (Freeze) and Book 2: Flame (Sets the enemy's space on fire). <br><br>

Out of all 5 effects, the initial effect of Freezing the foe from Book 1 is arguably the best. Freeze is an incredibly debilitating status effect that stops the target from moving. <br><br>

You can think of BoW as a "Charging Attack" where the longer you wait to use it without attacking, the more powerful it becomes. To get the full effect of BoW you need to wait 5 Turns which seems like a lot, but you speed this up with Dancing, either from your Dancer or Byleth's Goddess Dance to get multiple rounds of "Wait" in a single turn.


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

For a Bond 1 Sync Skill (i.e: The one skill anyone wearing Veronica's Bracelet will have access to), <b>Reprisal</b> is absolutely disgusting and its upgraded form doubles down on this. What makes Reprisal so powerful is the specific wording of the skill. It adds your lost HP to your ATTACK, not Damage. What this means is that if you get a Critical Hit, the attack bonus is also multiplied by 3 (or 4 if you have Brute Force from Chrom & Robin). If you put on your main Ike User who has the Vantage+Wrath Build, expect to see comically high damage numbers that will annihilate all but the bulkiest of enemies and even those will left on death's door. <br><br>

However, there is a little quirk with Reprisal that I need to discuss. When you have Reprisal equipped (either through Syncing with Veronica or by Skill Inheritance) you might notice the Combat Forecast looks a bit off. If you are doubling and the enemy can counterattack, you might notice that your 2nd Attack is predicted to do more damage than your 1st Attack. This is due to the game assuming the enemy's attack will connect, lower your HP, and thus boost the effect of Reprisal. However, if the enemy misses, expect your 2nd Attack to do the same damage as your 1st. <br><br>


<b>Level Boost</b> is another Sync Skill that is incredibly simple but powerful. Recall how Roy's Engage Skill, Rise Above grants you 5 Extra Levels along with the boosted stats from those levels. Veronica gives you a slightly watered-down version that isn't tied to her Engage Form. If Veronica's bearer is a good combat unit, expect them to max out this skill's effect early into a battle. <br><br>

<b>SP Conversion</b> is also disgusting as it lets you rack up SP insanely fast. Normally in Engage, to gain SP you have to have either an Emblem Ring/Bracelet or a Bong Ring equipped, and even when you defeat an enemy, you'll usually get around 10 SP per kill. This was designed as a way of balancing the SP and Skill Inheritance System so you have to think carefully about what you spend your SP on. Veronica comes along and laughs in this system's face.


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

<b>Contract</b> is basically refreshing an ally for another action with the caveat that the affected ally cannot move. However, the real beauty of Contract is that it can be combined with other Refresh effects like Dance, Goddess Dance, and Raging Storm. This is especially useful when taking on bosses as when you're fighting a boss, you'll end your turn within striking distance of them (assuming you aren't using Canter). So using Contract allows for a unit to attack a boss another time. If you recall back when I discussed the Emblem of Rivals, they can get multiple actions within the same turn. Contract adds yet another action on top of those. <br><br>

The Class Bonuses are nice. Dragons giving Stat Bonuses is great. Covert Units grant a Hit and Avoid bonus making the contracted unit more accurate and evasive. Backup Units get a special effect as Veronica's Bearer (The unit wearing her Bracelet) will Chain Attack during the contracted ally's combat. However, this is only for the action granted by Contract. <br><br>

If you think Veronica's Engage Skill is great, get ready for her Engage Attack.

</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Veronica brought with her the single most powerful thing in the Fire Emblem Multiverse: Gacha! How this works is that first, you choose a type of Summoning Stone that corresponds to a specific Weapon Type a unit wields (Red = Sword, Blue = Lances, Green = Axes, Colourless = Everything else). <br><br>

The Fabrications summoned by this attack can be nice but the real prizes are the 5-Star Emblems. These Emblems come with one of their Engage Weapons corresponding to whichever stone you choose and their entire kit as an Emblem (Sync Skills, Engage Skills, and Engage Attack). For example, if you have summoned Sigurd, he will have Canter, Momentum, Headlong Rush, Gallop, Override, and one of his Engage Weapons. <br><br>

Whatever you summon, they all share the following traits. <br>
- They are fully controllable like your regular units. <br>
- They last until either they die or Veronica's Engage wears off. <br>
- They cannot gain Experience. <br><br>

While you might think having an extra Unit on the field means more manpower, the real beauty of Summon Hero is that whatever you end up summoning is <b>100% EXPENDABLE.</b> If the Fabrication/Emblem dies, you lose absolutely nothing. There are tons of ways to take advantage of this but my personal favorite is luring out Bosses. <br><br>

In Engage, Bosses are stationary until you either aggro them (e.g.: through Lyn's Astra Storm) or you enter their attack range. You can send Veronica's Summon into the Boss' attack range to lure them towards your army without risking anyone. This is especially useful as (without getting into too many spoilers) during the Main Story of Engage, you will face Bosses wielding Emblems of their own and the first thing they like to do when something enters their attack range is use their Engage Attack. You can simply have Veronica's Summon take one for the team by taking that Engage Attack. <br><br>

For Class Bonuses, Dragons get the best deal as they completely cut 3-Stars out of the Summoning Pool and add their 30% Chance to the 4-Star Summon Chance. When summoned by a Backup Unit, the Summoned Unit will have the Dual Strike Skill... which is a bit useless as the vast majority of Emblems are Backup Units by default. Lastly, Cavalry Units simply give their Summoned Units a little more movement. 


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Continuing the trend of Veronica's kit being absurdly powerful, Veronica's Engage Weapons are no different as they are some level of broken and difficult to pronounce due to 2 of them being written in Old Norse. <br><br>

<b>Hliðskjálf</b> is a "Staff" but the game treats it as a Tome for combat purposes as Celica's Resonance does interact with it. Since the foe cannot counterattack you initiate combat, this is basically free damage on the enemy. This Staff is a reference to the Dazzling Staff skill in Fire Emblem Heroes where Staff Units equipped with it nullify the enemy's counterattack.  <br><br>

<b>Fortify+</b> is an objectively better version of the regular Fortify Staff. Not only does it heal more and have a wider range, but it is also a party-wide Restore staff that cleanses status ailments. Better yet, since this is an Engage Weapon, it will NEVER break, meaning you can use it as many times as you like.  <br><br>

Finally, we have Veronica's signature tome <b>Élivágar</b>. This tome has fantastic stats across the board but its real power lies in its effect. Remember how I said that Enemy Bosses in Engage will be rocking their own Emblems? As you've noticed, Emblems provide passive Stat bonuses when worn and the same concept applies to the enemy. Élivágar is your answer to this as simply using against an enemy with an Emblem, will neutralize ALL bonuses that Emblem provides, making this a fantastic way to open Boss Fights.  <br><br>

However, please remember that Élivágar does not nullify stat bonuses granted by terrain such as Miasma, unfortunately. 


</details>

<details>
<summary> Explanation </summary>

Veronica's <b>Heroes Engrave</b> is quite an interesting one. A weapon gains Attack, Avoid, Dodge, and much lower Weight at the hefty cost of -20 to Hit and Critical Rate. The obvious use case is putting this on either weapons with high base Hit Rates like Swords or Lances or using this Engrave on weapons used by characters with high Dexterity Stats. Alternatively, you could inherit a Hit+ Skill or Divine Pulse from Sigurd or Byleth. <br><br>

However, there is another option for this Engrave that I don't see discussed often; Surge Tomes. In Engage, the Surge and Elsurge Tomes are unique in that, they are strictly 1-Range, Melee Tomes. However, in exchange they <b>ALWAYS HIT</b>. The game says they have Infinite (∞) Hit but in the game's code, their HIT is coded as 255. Putting the Heroes Engrave on an Elsurge Tome gives it a whooping 15 Might and 5 Weight, making doubling with it incredibly easy. Having effectively infinite HIT means the HIT penalty from the Heroes Engrave becomes moot. The Critical Rate penalty is negligible as Surge Tomes have low base Critical Rates the penalty doesn't matter in the grand scheme of things. <br><br> 

Fun Fact: Veronica's Engrave is the only one that decreases a weapon's Hit. 

</details>

Veronica's Skill Inheritance is a mix of dirt cheap and incredibly expensive. Given she comes from a Gacha Game, this makes sense. If you want those premium 5-Star Skills, you better be ready to bust out the <s>Credit Card</s> Skill Books. <br><br>

<b>Reprisal</b> is the prize here and as to be expected of a skill so powerful, it is extremely expensive. Reprisal+ is hands down one of, if not the most expensive Inheritable Sync Skill. If you want this, I hope you've been using the Ancient Well a lot. If you want to put Reprisal on someone other than Veronica's bearer, I recommend putting it on your main Ike User, especially if they are running the Vantage+Wrath Build. These 3 Skills working together can create some absolutely ludicrous damage numbers.<br><br>

<b>Book of Worlds</b> is dirt cheap which is a bit surprising considering how many effects are crammed into it. For 300 SP, it might be worth playing around with on other Units in your army.<br><br>

<b>SP Conversion</b> is definitely worth it and don't even worry about that 300 SP price tag. With how much SP you'll be making, you'll make that 300 SP back in no time. Furthermore, if you do Veronica's Divine Paralogue early, this skill might be worth spreading around to get an early start on stockpiling SP.<br><br>

<b>Level Boost</b> is also a good investment, especially for 300 SP. Nothing more to say here.<br><br>

Veronica's Stat Skill is <b>Magic/Dexterity+</b> and as we've seen with Celica and Soren, any Stat Skill that directly boosts Magic (aka, your raw damage with Tomes) will be expensive. <br><br>

Her Guard Skill is <b>Knife Guard</b> which is more useful than you'd think. Enemy Knife-users like Thieves and Wolf Knights hit harder than you would think, so having the skill to buffer the damage is nice. <br><br>

Finally, Veronica gives <b>Staff and Tome Proficiencies</b>. Do I even need to say anything about Tome Proficiency here?

# **Character Builds**



<details>
<summary> Boss Neutering</summary>
This is my favorite way of running Veronica. Recall that in Engage, Bosses will also have Emblems and benefit from the passive Stat Bonuses they provide. Veronica's Élivágar is your answer to this as it will nullify those stat bonuses for 1 Turn. However, what if we could take this further? <br><br>

If you've read Corrin's Profile, you will remember how I gushed about how useful her Draconic Hex Sync Skill is. For a refresher, if a unit equipped with Draconic Hex initiates combat, the foe is inflicted with a -4 penalty to all of their basic stats, regardless of whether the attack did damage or even landed. <br><br>

You will be surprised to know that Elivagar and Draconic Hex STACK. Not only will you nullify the target's stat bonuses but also inflict massive stat penalties on them in one fell swoop. I cannot stress how much this completely neuters Bosses and makes them almost comically easy to kill. <br><br>

To pull off this build, I recommend having Veronica's main user inherit Draconic Hex from Corrin (which won't be hard with SP Conversion). Otherwise, keep your Veronica and Corrin users close by so they can tag-team the Boss and neuter their stats. 

</details>

<details>
<summary> Veronica fights for her Friends?</summary>

I mentioned how Reprisal, if inherited by an Ike User with the Vantage+Wrath Build can do insane amounts of Damage due to Reprisal and Wrath getting stronger, the lower your HP is. Who's to say Veronica's user can't benefit from that too? If you decide to use Veronica more offensively, I recommend having her main user inherit Vantage from Leif and Wrath from Ike. This shouldn't be too hard with SP Conversion and Skill Books.

</details>





# **Final Thoughts** <br>
For the last DLC Emblem of Engage, we have ended on a high note with Veronica. The DLC Emblems have been varying levels of overpowered but Veronica takes the cake. Every single piece of her kit is designed to break the game's balance and difficulty, either subtly or overtly. If you're doing your first playthrough of Maddening Difficulty and you decide to use one DLC Emblem, I highly recommend using Veronica. Even on Maddening, her Divine Paralogue isn't difficult, especially if you do it as soon as it unlocks and her abilities make Maddening much more bearable.
